. . . your devoted rebel.

. . . I lose no moment to complete Romney to satisfaction.

. . . Some say that Happiness is not good for Mortals, and they ought to be answered that sorrow is not good for Immortals; a blight never does good to a tree, and if a blight kill not a tree, but it shall bear fruit, let none say that the fruit was in consequence of the blight.
