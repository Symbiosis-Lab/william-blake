ocr_note: The whole note, written in the third person — a formal convention, not a lapse into someone else's voice. Keynes dates it only "October, 1791." Reveley (d. 1799), a young architect, had asked Blake to engrave plates after William Pars's drawings for Stuart and Revett's *Antiquities of Athens* (1794); Blake's note answers that request. The bracketed clause is his own cancelled wording, kept here as Keynes's diplomatic transcription shows it, from the original note (H. E. Huntington Library).

Mr Blake's Compts. to Mr Reveley; tho' full of work [as Mr R. said he should be by then, the plates were put in hand del.] he is glad to embrace the offer of engraving such beautiful things & will do what he can by the end of January.
