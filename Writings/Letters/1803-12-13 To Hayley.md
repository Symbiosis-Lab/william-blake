source: printed in Keynes, The Writings of William Blake (Nonesuch, 1925), vol. 2, letter XXXI. Public domain in the United States (the 1925 edition's US copyright term expired 1 January 2021). OCR via Internet Archive item writingsofwillia02blak.
ocr_note: Only this sentence survives, quoted in a sale catalogue; Keynes prints no salutation or signature. "This infernal business of the soldier" is the pending charge of sedition brought against Blake by the soldier John Scofield after the Felpham garden quarrel — he was tried and acquitted at Chichester the following month. The catalogue's further remark, that the letter "alludes to Romney and Flaxman, giving some interesting details," is its own paraphrase and is omitted here.
