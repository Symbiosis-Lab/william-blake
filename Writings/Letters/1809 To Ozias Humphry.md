Dear Sir,

You will see in this little work the cause of difference between you & me. You demand of me to Mix two things that Reynolds has confess'd cannot be mixed. You will percieve that I not only detest False Art, but have the Courage to say so Publickly & to dare all the Power on Earth to oppose — Florentine & Venetian Art cannot exist together. Till the Venetian & Flemish are destroy'd, the Florentine & Roman cannot Exist; this will be shortly accomplish'd; till then I remain your Grateful, altho' Seemingly otherwise, I say your Grateful & Sincere,

William Blake.

I inclose a ticket of admission if you should honour my Exhibition with a Visit.
