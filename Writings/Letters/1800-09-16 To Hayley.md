Lambeth,

16 Sept. 1800.

Leader of my Angels,

. . . I invoke the Good Genii that surround Miss Poole's Villa to shine upon my journey—whether I come on Wednesday or Thursday that Day shall be marked in my calendar with a star of first magnitude. Eartham will be my first temple & altar. . . . My Wife is like a flame of many colours of precious jewels whenever she hears it named. . . . My fingers emit sparks of fire with Expectation of my future labour. . . .
