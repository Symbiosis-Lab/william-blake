---
description: A fair-copy manuscript of ten short poems, prepared by Blake c. 1807, probably for B. H. Malkin or another patron. None of the ten were engraved. The manuscript descended through several hands before passing to the bookseller B. M. Pickering, whence its name; it is now at the Morgan Library, MA 1473.

The ten poems, in the manuscript's own order:

1. *[[The Smile]]*
2. *[[The Golden Net]]*
3. *[[The Mental Traveller]]*
4. *[[The Land of Dreams]]*
5. *[[Mary]]*
6. *[[The Crystal Cabinet]]*
7. *[[The Grey Monk]]*
8. *[[Auguries of Innocence]]*
9. *[[Long John Brown and Little Mary Bell]]*
10. *[[William Bond]]*

Sampson's edition prints a textual apparatus under each poem, collating his reading against Ellis & Yeats (EY), Rossetti (DGR), William Michael Rossetti (WMR), W. B. Yeats (WBY), and Shepherd (Shep.); that apparatus is not reproduced here.
