Blake worked on *Vala* from c. 1796 through 1807, writing and revising in pencil and ink on the same sheets, sometimes several layers deep. He never engraved it, and much of its matter was later carried, with substantial change, into *Milton* and *Jerusalem*.

Of the nine Nights, only the first is given here in full: *[[Night the First]]*.

Ellis & Yeats's edition does not mark clean section breaks between Nights II–VIII, and their text is too heavily emended for a page-by-page extraction to be trusted without a modern critical edition (Bentley 1963, Erdman 1988) at hand — both still in copyright. Night the Ninth (*Being the Last Judgment*) is likewise not given here as verse: the research behind this site found only Ellis & Yeats's textual apparatus to that Night, not a clean run of its lines; see *[[Night the Ninth]]* for what that page can honestly say.
