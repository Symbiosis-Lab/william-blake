He began the manuscript in the mid-1790s as a mythological epic in nine Nights, working and reworking the same sheets in pencil and ink for a decade without ever bringing it to completion or to the press.

Much of its matter was later carried, with substantial change, into *Milton* and *Jerusalem*.
