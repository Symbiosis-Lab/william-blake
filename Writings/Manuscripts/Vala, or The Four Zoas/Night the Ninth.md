Of the nine Nights of *Vala, or The Four Zoas*, this site gives only the first in full — *[[Night the First]]*. Night the Ninth, *Being the Last Judgment*, the longest and most apocalyptic of the nine, is not given here as verse.

Ellis & Yeats print *Vala* as a continuous run without clean section breaks between most Nights, and the material found for this site's research was limited to their textual apparatus to Night the Ninth (the collation of Blake's manuscript revisions, vol. III pp. 174ff) rather than a clean, page-checked run of the Night's own lines. Printing that apparatus in place of the poem would misrepresent it as Blake's verse, so this page states the gap instead.

For the text of Night the Ninth, see:

- David V. Erdman, ed., *The Complete Poetry and Prose of William Blake* (Anchor, rev. 1988), pp. 386–407. In copyright.
- G. E. Bentley Jr., ed., *Vala, or The Four Zoas: A Facsimile of the Manuscript* (Oxford, 1963). In copyright.
