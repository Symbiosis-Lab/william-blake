## Comus, 1801 and 1815

Eight watercolours commissioned by the Rev. Joseph Thomas in 1801 (now the Huntington Library), and a second set of eight commissioned by Thomas Butts in 1815 (now the Museum of Fine Arts, Boston) — the two complete surviving sets of designs to Milton's masque.

## Paradise Lost, 1807, 1808, and c. 1822
