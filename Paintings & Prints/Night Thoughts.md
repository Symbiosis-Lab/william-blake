The title-plate for Night the First reproduces Young's own subtitle for that Night, "Night the First, on Life, Death and Immortality" — Blake engraved the design, but the words are the poet's, repeated at the head of each of the nine Nights. Blake mentioned the commission once in a letter to George Cumberland, 26 August 1799: "since my Young's Night Thoughts have been published, even Johnson and Fuseli have discarded my graver" (Russell 1906, pp. 66–67). Eight plates from Nights the First through the Fourth follow.
