Blake illustrated the Book of Job twice in watercolour before he ever cut the plates reproduced on the [[Illustrations of the Book of Job]] page. The first set was made for Thomas Butts, probably around 1805–1806; the second, closely following the first, was drawn in 1821 for John Linnell, who went on to commission the engravings from it. The twenty-one subjects are the same in both sets and in the same order.

## The Linnell set

Commissioned and acquired by John Linnell in 1821; dispersed from his estate. Twenty of the twenty-one sheets reproduced here are catalogued as the Linnell set (Butlin 551); the twentieth subject, *Job's Evil Dreams*, is the one sheet of the set held apart, at the Fogg Art Museum, Harvard.
