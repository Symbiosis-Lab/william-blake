Milton exists in several copies, and Blake reworked it between 1804 and 1811: the copy digitised here (Copy D, Library of Congress) carries more plates than the earlier copies the 1904 edition below was set from, and a handful of its plates fall outside that edition's own plate count. Where that happens the plate and the text section carrying its number are not the same etched page — the plates are still shown in Copy D's own order, and the text is complete and unabridged, but the two should be read as two views of the poem rather than a caption pointing at its picture.

- [[Preface]] — Plate 2, and the hymn "And did those feet in ancient time."
- [[Book the First]] — Plates 3–28 in the 1904 edition's count.
- [[Book the Second]] — Plates 30–45 in the 1904 edition's count.
